# 🪙 VibeHired Credit System Guide

This guide outlines how credits (coins) are consumed within the VibeHired application and which AI features are currently available for free.

## 💰 Coin-Based AI Features
The following features require credits to use. Credits are deducted from your balance based on the complexity and resource intensity of the AI operation.

| Feature | Cost (Coins) | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Chat Messages** | 1 | Interacting with the AI assistant for advice or help. |
| **Job Extraction** | 1 | Automatically extracting job details from a URL or pasted text. |
| **Email Lead Scan** | 1 | Scanning your Gmail for potential job leads or application updates. |
| **ATS Scoring & Scan** | 2 | Analyzing your CV against a job description for Applicant Tracking System compatibility. |
| **CV Analysis & Scoring** | 2 | Detailed breakdown of your CV with section-by-section improvement suggestions. |
| **CV Parsing** | 2 | Extracting professional history and skills from an uploaded PDF or Word document. |
| **CV Generation** | 3 | Using AI to improve sections, apply ATS suggestions, or generate a tailored CV. |
| **Cover Letter Generation** | 3 | Crafting a customized cover letter tailored specifically to a job description. |
| **Interview Simulation** | 3 | Generating mock interview questions and providing feedback on your answers. |
| **Auto-Jobs Workflow** | 0.1 / job | Automated discovery and analysis (e.g., 100 jobs costs 10 credits). |

> [!NOTE]
> The **Auto-Jobs Workflow** no longer requires you to provide your own Apify API key. We handle the integration server-side, and you only pay for the jobs you choose to retrieve in your settings.

---

## 🎁 Free AI Features
These AI-powered features are currently integrated into the app but **do not consume any coins**.

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Base Cover Letter Extraction** | When you upload a previous cover letter as a "base," AI automatically extracts its text so you can use it for tailoring. |
| **Interview Material Naming** | AI suggests descriptive titles for uploaded interview preparation files or notes. |
| **Work Tracker: Magic Prompt** | Logs shifts or appointments simply by speaking or typing natural sentences (e.g., *"Add a 9-to-5 shift at Google next Tuesday"*). |
| **Work Tracker: Schedule Import** | Automatically parses images or PDFs of work schedules and turns them into calendar entries. |

---

## 📈 Plan Comparison
Your monthly credit allowance depends on your subscription plan:

*   **Free Trial**: 20 Coins (One-time)
*   **Starter**: 150 Coins / month
*   **Pro**: 500 Coins / month
*   **Premium**: 1,500 Coins / month

> [!TIP]
> You can check your remaining balance at the bottom of the sidebar at any time. Credits for staff and test accounts may appear high, but usage is still tracked to ensure system integrity.
